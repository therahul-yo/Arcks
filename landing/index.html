Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

01
Instant previews
Hover triggers after 350ms — fast enough to feel instant, slow enough to not fire on accident.
02
Dual AI providers
Choose between Gemini 2.5 Flash and any OpenRouter model. Swap without touching the extension.
03
Server-side fetching
Pages are fetched by a Cloudflare Worker — no CORS limits, no client-side scraping, no fingerprinting.
04
Two-layer cache
KV cache at 30 min on the worker + local cache at 10 min in the extension. Most hovers cost zero API calls.
05
Privacy first
No browsing history stored anywhere. Summaries are generated ephemerally and never logged.
06
Share built in
Every card has a native share button — tap to share via system sheet or copy to clipboard as fallback.
