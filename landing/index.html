Features

Built for fast, private browsing.

Everything you need to skip dead links and find what you're actually looking for — without sacrificing speed or privacy.

Instant previews

Hover triggers after 350ms — fast enough to feel instant, slow enough to never fire by accident.

AI-powered

Choose between Gemini 2.5 Flash or any OpenRouter model. Swap providers without touching the extension.

Worker-fetched

Pages are fetched by a Cloudflare Worker — no CORS limits, no client-side scraping, no fingerprinting.

Two-layer cache

KV cache on the worker (30m) plus a local cache in the extension (10m). Most hovers cost zero API calls.

Privacy-first

No browsing history stored anywhere. Summaries are generated ephemerally and never logged.

One-tap share

Every card has a native share button — uses the system share sheet, falls back to clipboard automatically.

How it works

From hover to preview in three steps.

1

Hover any Google result

Arcks watches your search results and fires after a 350ms hover — debounced so it doesn't trigger on flyovers.

2

Worker fetches and summarizes

The Cloudflare Worker fetches the page, sends a stripped version to Gemini or OpenRouter, and streams the response back.

3

Preview card renders instantly

A structured card appears beside the link — headline, three bullets, and a share button. Cached for next time.
